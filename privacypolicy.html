Privacy Policy – Artificia Intellec Pvt Ltds

Last Updated: August 15, 2025
Website: www.artificiaai.com

At Artificia Intellec Pvt Ltd (“Artificia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and safeguard the data of students, candidates, clients, and website users.
By using our services, enrolling in training, or engaging with our recruitment solutions, you (“User,” “Student,” “Candidate,” “Client”) agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on whether you are a student, job seeker, or client company.

1.1 Personal Identification Information:
● Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender
● Residential or business address.
● Educational and professional background.

1.2 Academic & Training Data:
● Enrollment forms, course progress, attendance records.
● Assignments, projects, assessments, certifications.
● Feedback, recorded sessions, learning analytics.

1.3 Recruitment & Employment Data:
● Resumes, LinkedIn profiles, cover letters, skill assessments.
● Interview recordings, employer feedback.
● Current and expected salary details.
● Employment history and references.

1.4 Technical & Usage Data:
● IP address, browser type, device details.
● Website analytics (pages visited, time spent).
● Cookies, tracking pixels, CRM interactions.

1.5 Financial & Payment Information:
● Billing address, bank/payment details (via secure gateways)
● Transaction history of courses or recruitment fees

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business, educational, and recruitment purposes.

2.1 Training Services:
● Process enrollments and verify eligibility
● Deliver courses, assignments, and progress tracking
● Provide student support, certifications, and career guidance
● Improve course content and personalize learning experiences

2.2 Recruitment Services:
● Match candidates with suitable job opportunities
● Share candidate profiles with verified employers
● Communicate interview schedules, feedback, and outcomes
● Maintain placement records for compliance and future opportunities

2.3 Marketing & Communication:
● Send updates, offers, newsletters, and promotional campaigns
● Notify users about new courses, events, and recruitment drives
● Display testimonials, case studies, and success stories (with consent)

2.4 Compliance & Legal Obligations:
● Fulfill contractual commitments with clients and students
● Prevent fraud, misuse, and violations of our terms
● Comply with Indian IT Act, 2000, GDPR, and applicable laws

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. However, we may share it under limited circumstances:
With Employers/Clients – Candidate profiles and resumes are shared with hiring companies (only after consent).
With Trainers & Mentors – Limited student data may be shared with faculty for progress evaluation.
With Service Providers – Payment gateways, cloud hosting, CRM, and email marketing tools (bound by confidentiality).
With Legal Authorities – If required under law, regulation, or court order.
With Affiliates/Partners – Only for delivering value-added services, never without consent.

4. Data Storage & Security:

● Data is stored securely on encrypted servers and cloud systems.
● Access is restricted to authorized employees only.
● We use firewalls, SSL encryption, 2FA, and secure databases.
● Payment data is processed only through PCI-DSS compliant gateways (we do not store card details).

5. Data Retention:

Students: Data retained for 5 years after course completion (for certification verification & alumni support).
Candidates: Recruitment data retained for 3 years unless deletion is requested.
Clients: Business records retained as long as legally required for compliance.
Website Analytics: Retained for 24 months.
Users may request erasure or anonymization of their data anytime, subject to legal obligations.

6. User Rights:

You have the following rights under this Privacy Policy:
Right to access – request a copy of your stored data
Right to correction – update or correct inaccuracies
Right to deletion – request erasure of personal data
Right to restrict processing – limit how your data is used
Right to withdraw consent – opt out of marketing anytime
Right to data portability – request transfer of your data to another platform

7. Cookies & Tracking:

● We use cookies and tracking tools to improve user experience and analyze site performance.
● You may disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work properly.
● Third-party tools (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insights) may track interactions for ad targeting.

8. Children’s Privacy:

● Students under 18 must provide parental/guardian consent for enrollment.
● Parents will receive progress reports and project updates.
● We do not knowingly collect or share data of minors without consent.

9. International Data Transfers:

As a global training and recruitment institute, your data may be transferred outside India. We ensure:
● Compliance with GDPR standards for EU residents
● Transfers only to countries with adequate data protection laws
● Binding contracts with service providers to maintain confidentiality

10. Limitation of Liability:

● Artificia is not liable for breaches caused by third-party hacks, user negligence, or force majeure events.
● We are not responsible for outcomes based on employer decisions or student misuse of tools.
● Our maximum liability is limited to the fees paid for the services.

11. Changes to Privacy Policy:

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on the website, and users are encouraged to review periodically. Continued use of our services implies acceptance of updated terms.
