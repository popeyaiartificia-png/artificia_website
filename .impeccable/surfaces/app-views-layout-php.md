# Visitor pages: section library and layout

Scope: every public page of artificiaai.com, built from the 17-section library (app/sections/*.php), the shared layout, and the /_design gallery. Visitor mode: Persuade. The admin area is a separate Operate surface and does not inherit this brief.

Audience and action: individuals from Meta ads on phones choose the masterclass; business buyers from Google choose corporate training or a built system. The action is the enquiry form for their door (or WhatsApp). Proof: Orkay Tiles case study, six real testimonials, "100+ professionals trained through corporate training sessions", five typical outcomes. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no prices, dates, superlatives; mobile budgets; reduced motion).

Memorable moment: lime marker sketches of real systems draw themselves as you read, and each section you finish takes a tick in the margin.

Unresolved: real classroom photography does not exist yet; the design must look complete with sketches and the six people photos only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the working notebook of a Vadodara team that builds AI systems and teaches them; each page opens at the entry that answers the visitor's question. It refuses the AI-course template: glow, gradients, robot imagery, a centred hero over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal #2A2E2E pages ruled with an 18px graph grid one step lighter, #08080d binding for nav, footer and form plates, off-white #F2F3EE ink, lime #AED534 used only as marker: sketch strokes, ticks, the primary action. Every grey is a step on one charcoal ramp. Roboto Flex (the Brand Bible's Roboto): expanded heavy caps for headings, normal width for reading, tabular figures for numbers. Square 2px corners. Lime single-stroke SVG drawings with leader-line callouts and a margin rule. Raises: form results write themselves as a new notebook line (from the phosphor terminal); one stepped grey ramp (exposure record); each drawing takes its question's form (Du Bois); a fixed entry header on every module, case and quote (brine calendar); one drawing type per offer (sleeping city); one lime completion tick (woodcut identity).

STORY: The visitor sees practitioners' drawings of real systems, understands what they would learn or get, picks their door, and enquires; the reply line appears in the notebook.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: binding bar (the Artificia logo image, Enquire, menu); headline in expanded caps over four or five lines, second clause in lime; a flow sketch drawing itself; three ruled chooser rows (Learn AI, Train my team, Build a system) as the primary action. Desktop: a two-page spread, headline and chooser on the left page, the sketch with callouts on the right.

FORM: The Build Notebook, candidate 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:0cba571798147367.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
